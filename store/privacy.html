Privacy Policy — SEO Meta Writer

For the “SEO Meta Writer” GPT on ChatGPT · Last updated: 27 October 2026

SEO Meta Writer is a custom GPT that writes SEO meta titles and meta descriptions from the page topic, content, keyword, or URL you provide in the conversation.

What data we collect

None. The GPT does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. It only reads the text you type into the conversation in order to generate meta tags. It runs no custom Actions and calls no third-party APIs of its own.

How your input is handled

Your conversation with the GPT takes place inside ChatGPT and is processed by OpenAI under OpenAI's Privacy Policy. The GPT's author does not receive, see, or store your conversations. If ChatGPT's web browsing is used to read a URL you provide, only that public page is fetched to ground the suggestions.

Data sharing

We do not sell or transfer any user data to third parties, and we use no data for any purpose beyond generating the meta tags you ask for.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same location with a new “Last updated” date.
